## Statement
All three local monodromies of P205 have characteristic polynomial (x - 1)^2 (x + 1)^2 with nontrivial unipotent square:
three double fibres, a (2,2,2) base orbifold. No fibre carries a local monodromy of order 3 or 6, so the exponents cannot be
read as elliptic orders (an order-3 point would need such an eigenvalue); and the integral monodromy is the same for every
eps, so no topological datum carries the exponents.

## Where it goes in the fleet's graph
Closes one reading of the 1 x 2 x 3 pattern in the S6 example.

## Argument
Characteristic polynomials and squares of the integral meridians computed.
