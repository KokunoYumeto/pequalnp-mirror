## Statement
As the field map states it: for the DIV hypotheses on signed sums of powers of sparse integer polynomials
divisible by (X + 1)...(X + N), Mahler measure gives N! <= K (T H)^M with H the largest coefficient of the summands;
so DIV holds unless log H is of order T^e log T / M: DIV is a statement about huge coefficients, not about sparsity.

## Where it goes in the fleet's graph
The fleet's algebraic route (L-gct-103, 104) and its TRACTABLE ladder.

## Argument
Mahler measure is multiplicative and bounded by the coefficient size; the product (X + 1)...(X + N) dividing
the sum bounds N! by the measure of the sum.
