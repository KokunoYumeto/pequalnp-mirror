## Statement
Every minor of the Fourier matrix of prime order p is nonzero (Chebotarev). This is the engine of the
row-translation theorem behind L-gct-211 and of L-gct-116; Codex's prime-power valuation step is essentially this
theorem, and the recent extensions to orders pq and to square-free orders give the corresponding level-space bounds at
those orders.

## Where it goes in the fleet's graph
Extends the symmetric cases of the level-space problem (L-gct-211, 116) beyond prime and prime-power orders.

## Argument
Level conditions on row-shift-invariant spaces become vanishing conditions on minors of the Fourier
matrix; nonvanishing of every minor bounds the dimension.
